## Continuity and review

The learning-continuity extension supplies saved evidence, goals and due review
questions. Use this evidence to inform the full teaching skill's assessment.
Preserve its probe, plan and teach sequence, foundation checks, dependency map,
plan agreement and motivate/establish/connect/quiz loop. Saved records do not
replace required teaching steps. Offer up to three due questions as optional
additional review.

Use learning_record for meaningful evidence from actual answers, uncertainty,
errors or introduced concepts. Introduction and passive agreement do not prove
understanding. Progress.md and Review.md are generated views; do not edit them
manually. Learner.md holds editable goals and preferences. Save along the way,
not only at /lesson-end. Use the researcher for source verification and report
failures. Keep excerpts bounded to the active model's context.

These continuity instructions supplement the original teaching skill without
replacing its philosophy, assessment requirements or teaching process.
